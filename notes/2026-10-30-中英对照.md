## Part B · 原文整段

**〔B.1｜英文原声：问一个古希腊人〕**

If you went to an ancient Greek, if you, if you went to Athens in four hundred BC, and you said to an ancient Greek, “What is love? And where is the role of criticism?” They would say, “Love is a process of education. It's the education of emotion, and lovers should of course be able to pick each other up for things that they don't spot in one another, and they should be trying to develop into the best version of themselves rather than simply staying stuck in an admiration for who they are today.”

> 如果你去一个古希腊，如果你，如果你在公元前四百年去过雅典，你问一位古希腊人："什么是爱？那么，批评的作用在哪里呢？"他们会说："爱是一个学习的过程。这是情感的教育，恋人们应该当然，他们也能够互相提醒对方注意那些自己没有发现的问题。另一个，他们应该努力发展成为最好的版本。与其仅仅沉浸在对他们身份的钦佩之中，不如去了解他们自己。"

**〔B.2｜中文旁白：选择与能力〕**

In the past, many people's marriages were mainly composed of family wealth and social status. To be able to say that I should have the right to choose who I love — certainly a liberation. But freedom of choice does not automatically bring the ability to love. We treat two things as the same thing. We believe we have the right to follow our heart, and gradually believe that our heart will replace us and make the right judgement: as long as the feeling is strong enough, this person is worth choosing.

> 过去许多人的婚姻主要由家族财产和社会地位决定。能够说出"我应该有权选择自己爱的人"，当然是一种解放。但选择的自由并没有自动带来相爱的能力。两件事当成了同一件事。我们相信自己有权跟随内心，也就渐渐相信内心会替我们做出正确的判断：只要感觉足够强烈，这个人就值得选择。

**〔B.3｜中文旁白：磨合如何变成证据〕**

Just choose the right one. Getting along shouldn't be so difficult. Therefore, many original needs in the relationship — the problem of understanding and running-in — has become evidence that love is in trouble.

> 只要选择正确，相处就不该那么困难。于是关系里许多原本需要理解和磨合的问题，都变成了爱情出了问题的证据。
