## Part A｜逐句对照（英文为译写）

### A.1

**EN** Why do some people only fall into frantic infatuation when they are utterly frozen out?

> **中** 为什么有些人只有在被彻底冷落时才会疯狂心动？

**EN** In his dynamics of desire, Lacan once tore away the false mask of this phenomenon.

> **中** 拉康在欲望的动力学中，曾撕开过这一现象的虚妄面具。

**EN** The engine that drives human desire has never been fullness — it is lack.

> **中** 人类欲望运转的底层引擎，从不是充盈，而是匮乏。

### A.2

**EN** In Lacan's system, desire is sustained only by an unmet gap.

> **中** 在拉康的体系里，欲望的维系高度依赖于一个未被满足的缺口。

**EN** When someone is completely compliant with you, available at any moment, the certainty of the relationship fills that gap in.

> **中** 当一个人对你百依百顺、随时可得时，关系的确定性便填平了那个缺口。

**EN** Desire loses the foothold it was climbing toward, and drifts of itself into exhaustion and boredom.

> **中** 欲望失去了攀爬的支点，自然会走向枯竭与倦怠。

**EN** And only when the other person pulls away entirely and the coldness opens up once more an abyss you cannot cross,

> **中** 而唯有当对方彻底抽离、冷落，重新拉开一道无法逾越的深渊时，

**EN** does the unobtainable object get deified in the subconscious and erupt into an almost deranged pull.

> **中** 那个得不到的客体，才会在潜意识中被骤然神化，爆发出近乎癫狂的吸引力。

### A.3

**EN** Roland Barthes, in A Lover's Discourse, saw straight through the absurd core of this psychology.

> **中** 罗兰·巴特在《恋人絮语》中，更是洞穿了这种心理的荒谬本质。

**EN** Barthes points out that a lover's most feverish infatuation rarely happens in the actual embrace — it happens in the other's absence.

> **中** 罗兰·巴特指出：恋人最狂热的迷恋，往往不发生在现实的相拥之中，而发生在对方的缺席里。

### A.4

**EN** That back turned on you in coldness becomes, in the inner world, a vast blank screen.

> **中** 那个对你冷漠退场的背影，在精神世界里化作了一面巨大的空白幕布。

**EN** To resist the emptiness and passivity that being frozen out brings, your psychic apparatus has to run at overload, calling up every last scrap of libido and hallucination to fill the vacuum.

> **中** 为了抵抗被冷落带来的虚无与被动，你的精神装置不得不超负荷运转，疯狂调动全部的力比多与幻觉去填补这个真空。

**EN** You think that in that moment you have finally recognised your own true heart.

> **中** 你以为你在那一刻终于认清了自己的真心，

**EN** But in fact you have merely staged a self-hypnotising revel of devotion, there in the white space of the other's coldness.

> **中** 但实际上，你只是在对方的冷漠留白中，完成了一场自我催眠的深情狂欢。

### A.5

**EN** This infatuation, sparked by coldness, is at bottom a stress response to a wound to omnipotent narcissism.

> **中** 这种由冷落激发出来的心动，本质上是一场全能自恋受挫后的应激反应。

**EN** Many people, in intimate relationships, cannot confirm their own worth inside an equal and gentle connection.

> **中** 许多人在亲密关系中，无法在平等且温和的连接里确认自我价值。

**EN** When the other person throws themselves in wholeheartedly, you find it insipid, even suffocating.

> **中** 当对方全心投入时，你感到索然无味，甚至感到窒息；

**EN** And the moment they withdraw all their goodwill, pushing you out to the position of onlooker, the safety deep inside you collapses in an instant.

> **中** 而一旦对方收回所有的善意，将你彻底推向旁观者的位置，你内心深处的安全感便瞬间坍塌。

**EN** That surge of losing control, and the terror of being abandoned, stabs at the arrogance in your subconscious that believes it ought to be gazed at unconditionally by everyone.

> **中** 那种扑面而来的失控感与被抛弃的恐慌，会刺痛你潜意识里理应被所有人无条件注视的傲慢。

### A.6

**EN** To quiet the shame and the defeat, your brain quickly reframes unwillingness to let go as an incurable love.

> **中** 你的大脑为了平息这份羞耻与挫败，迅速将不甘心重构为不可自拔的爱意。

**EN** The object of your frantic infatuation is never that cold body.

> **中** 你疯狂心动的对象，从来不是那个冷漠的肉身，

**EN** It is your own shattered self-esteem, the one that urgently needs redeeming through reconquest.

> **中** 而是你那份急需通过重新征服来救赎的破碎自尊。

**EN** Under the name of pursuit, you try to wipe out the humiliation of having been ignored.

> **中** 你借着追逐的名义，试图抹平自己被忽视的耻辱。

### A.7

**EN** Yet seeing through the illusion beneath infatuation is not so that you turn cold on all intimacy.

> **中** 然而，看清心动的底层假象，不是为了对一切亲密关系心灰意冷，

**EN** It is so that you wake fully from the masochistic loop that lack drives.

> **中** 而是为了从匮乏驱动的受虐循环中彻底清醒。

**EN** Mistaking losing control for passion, mistaking coldness for devotion — that is an emotional dislocation the mind forms over a long training.

> **中** 把失控当成激情，把冷落当成深情，是心智在漫长规训中形成的一种情感错位。

### A.8

**EN** Real love never needs the sensation of falling from a cliff edge to prove itself.

> **中** 真正的爱，从来不需要依靠悬崖边缘的坠落感来证明，

**EN** Still less does it need the other's cruelty as fuel for the engine of your own desire.

> **中** 更不需要用对方的残忍来给自己的欲望机器当燃料。〔原文句末存疑，见文末说明〕

**EN** Try to see through this desire-game you wrote and staged yourself.

> **中** 试着看破这场自导自演的欲望游戏，

**EN** Stop the hands that keep trying to beg warmth from coldness.

> **中** 停下那双试图向冷漠乞求温度的手，

**EN** Learn to tell pathological self-esteem lashing back apart from a healthy living connection.

> **中** 学会区分病态的自尊反扑与健康的生命连接。

**EN** Let loss happen, and let some people simply not belong to you.

> **中** 允许失去发生，也允许有些人就是不属于你。

### A.9

**EN** Only when you have utterly let go of your pathological attachment to lack and suspense, and learned to enjoy what is plain, steady and respected,

> **中** 唯有当你彻底放下了对匮乏与悬念的病态迷恋，学会享受平实、安定与被尊重时，

**EN** can you truly step down from this self-deceiving emotional treadmill, and live out a true self that is clear-eyed, self-sufficient and genuinely warm.

> **中** 你才能真正从这场自欺欺人的情感游戏中走下来，活出通透、自足且拥有真实温度的本真自我。〔原文两处存疑，见文末说明〕

## Part B｜整段对照（英文为译写）

### B.1

Why do some people only fall into frantic infatuation when they are utterly frozen out? In his dynamics of desire, Lacan once tore away the false mask of this phenomenon. The engine that drives human desire has never been fullness — it is lack. In Lacan's system, desire is sustained only by an unmet gap.

> 为什么有些人只有在被彻底冷落时才会疯狂心动？拉康在欲望的动力学中，曾撕开过这一现象的虚妄面具。人类欲望运转的底层引擎，从不是充盈，而是匮乏。在拉康的体系里，欲望的维系高度依赖于一个未被满足的缺口。

### B.2

When someone is completely compliant with you, available at any moment, the certainty of the relationship fills that gap in. Desire loses the foothold it was climbing toward, and drifts of itself into exhaustion and boredom. And only when the other person pulls away entirely and the coldness opens up once more an abyss you cannot cross, does the unobtainable object get deified in the subconscious and erupt into an almost deranged pull. Roland Barthes, in A Lover's Discourse, saw straight through the absurd core of this psychology. Barthes points out that a lover's most feverish infatuation rarely happens in the actual embrace — it happens in the other's absence.

> 当一个人对你百依百顺、随时可得时，关系的确定性便填平了那个缺口。欲望失去了攀爬的支点，自然会走向枯竭与倦怠。而唯有当对方彻底抽离、冷落，重新拉开一道无法逾越的深渊时，那个得不到的客体，才会在潜意识中被骤然神化，爆发出近乎癫狂的吸引力。罗兰·巴特在《恋人絮语》中，更是洞穿了这种心理的荒谬本质。罗兰·巴特指出：恋人最狂热的迷恋，往往不发生在现实的相拥之中，而发生在对方的缺席里。

### B.3

That back turned on you in coldness becomes, in the inner world, a vast blank screen. To resist the emptiness and passivity that being frozen out brings, your psychic apparatus has to run at overload, calling up every last scrap of libido and hallucination to fill the vacuum. You think that in that moment you have finally recognised your own true heart. But in fact you have merely staged a self-hypnotising revel of devotion, there in the white space of the other's coldness. This infatuation, sparked by coldness, is at bottom a stress response to a wound to omnipotent narcissism.

> 那个对你冷漠退场的背影，在精神世界里化作了一面巨大的空白幕布。为了抵抗被冷落带来的虚无与被动，你的精神装置不得不超负荷运转，疯狂调动全部的力比多与幻觉去填补这个真空。你以为你在那一刻终于认清了自己的真心，但实际上，你只是在对方的冷漠留白中，完成了一场自我催眠的深情狂欢。这种由冷落激发出来的心动，本质上是一场全能自恋受挫后的应激反应。

### B.4

Many people, in intimate relationships, cannot confirm their own worth inside an equal and gentle connection. When the other person throws themselves in wholeheartedly, you find it insipid, even suffocating. And the moment they withdraw all their goodwill, pushing you out to the position of onlooker, the safety deep inside you collapses in an instant. That surge of losing control, and the terror of being abandoned, stabs at the arrogance in your subconscious that believes it ought to be gazed at unconditionally by everyone. To quiet the shame and the defeat, your brain quickly reframes unwillingness to let go as an incurable love.

> 许多人在亲密关系中，无法在平等且温和的连接里确认自我价值。当对方全心投入时，你感到索然无味，甚至感到窒息；而一旦对方收回所有的善意，将你彻底推向旁观者的位置，你内心深处的安全感便瞬间坍塌。那种扑面而来的失控感与被抛弃的恐慌，会刺痛你潜意识里理应被所有人无条件注视的傲慢。你的大脑为了平息这份羞耻与挫败，迅速将不甘心重构为不可自拔的爱意。

### B.5

The object of your frantic infatuation is never that cold body. It is your own shattered self-esteem, the one that urgently needs redeeming through reconquest. Under the name of pursuit, you try to wipe out the humiliation of having been ignored. Yet seeing through the illusion beneath infatuation is not so that you turn cold on all intimacy. It is so that you wake fully from the masochistic loop that lack drives. Mistaking losing control for passion, mistaking coldness for devotion — that is an emotional dislocation the mind forms over a long training.

> 你疯狂心动的对象，从来不是那个冷漠的肉身，而是你那份急需通过重新征服来救赎的破碎自尊。你借着追逐的名义，试图抹平自己被忽视的耻辱。然而，看清心动的底层假象，不是为了对一切亲密关系心灰意冷，而是为了从匮乏驱动的受虐循环中彻底清醒。把失控当成激情，把冷落当成深情，是心智在漫长规训中形成的一种情感错位。

### B.6

Real love never needs the sensation of falling from a cliff edge to prove itself. Still less does it need the other's cruelty as fuel for the engine of your own desire. Try to see through this desire-game you wrote and staged yourself. Stop the hands that keep trying to beg warmth from coldness. Learn to tell pathological self-esteem lashing back apart from a healthy living connection. Let loss happen, and let some people simply not belong to you. Only when you have utterly let go of your pathological attachment to lack and suspense, and learned to enjoy what is plain, steady and respected, can you truly step down from this self-deceiving emotional treadmill, and live out a true self that is clear-eyed, self-sufficient and genuinely warm.

> 真正的爱，从来不需要依靠悬崖边缘的坠落感来证明，更不需要用对方的残忍来给自己的欲望机器当燃料。试着看破这场自导自演的欲望游戏，停下那双试图向冷漠乞求温度的手，学会区分病态的自尊反扑与健康的生命连接。允许失去发生，也允许有些人就是不属于你。唯有当你彻底放下了对匮乏与悬念的病态迷恋，学会享受平实、安定与被尊重时，你才能真正从这场自欺欺人的情感游戏中走下来，活出通透、自足且拥有真实温度的本真自我。
