## Part B · 原文整段

**〔V1｜瘾是同一个问题的不同出口〕**

When you're not doing what you really want to do, you pick up addictions — to smoking, to drinking, to sex. You binge-eat, you scroll endlessly, you play video games, you go looking for any kind of thrill. These look like different problems, but they're really different exits from the same one.
> 当你没有在做自己真正想做的事情时，你会染上烟瘾、酒瘾、性瘾。你会暴饮暴食，你会不停地刷手机、打游戏，甚至去寻找各种刺激。这些东西看似是不同的问题，其实是同一个问题的不同出口。

**〔V1｜那些瘾是盖住焦虑的盖子〕**

You're not doing what you actually want to do — you're running away, and somewhere inside you know you're running away, so a kind of anxiety builds up that you can't even name. Those addictions, that inner churn — they're all just lids you put over the anxiety. So don't overthink it. Skip past everything that's blocking you, and just do it. Even if it's only ten minutes today — once you start, the addictions recede on their own.
> 你没有在做自己真正想做的事，你在逃避，但内心知道自己在逃避，于是产生了一种自己都不知道的焦虑。那些瘾、那些内耗，全都是用来盖住焦虑的盖子。所以你要做的，就是别想那么多，跳过所有阻碍你的东西，直接做。哪怕今天只做 10 分钟，你一旦开始做了，那些瘾会自己退潮。

**〔V2｜挽留的代价与真正的输〕**

If someone wants to leave, let them leave. Don't beg, don't chase, don't cling, and don't trade your dignity for a comeback that was never guaranteed. Letting someone go doesn't mean you lost. Clinging to someone who doesn't love you, grinding yourself down until you're barely human — that's the real loss.
> 人家要走你就让人家走，别求、别追、别纠缠，别再拿尊严去换一个根本不确定的回头。成全对方不是你输了；死死抓着一个不爱你的人，把自己折磨得人不像人——那才是真的输啊。

**〔V2｜把清单一件一件捡回来〕**

Open the door and let the ones who want to go go. Loosen your grip and let go of what was never yours. Then pick your dignity back up, your life, your emotions, and that version of yourself you lost so long ago — one piece at a time. They've already decided they don't want this relationship. Are you really going to decide you don't want yourself either?
> 门打开，让想走的人走；手松开，让不属于你的东西离开。然后把你的尊严、你的生活、你的情绪，还有那个被你弄丢很久的自己，一个一个地捡回来。对方都已经选择不要这段关系了，你还打算连自己也不要了吗？

**〔V3｜扔掉过去，直接开始〕**

No explanations, just switch identity. Your past needs no fixing, just throw it all away. Start taking a new action at once. Step into a new role directly.
> 别解释，直接换身份。你的过去不需要修复，把它全部扔掉就好。立刻开始一个新的行动，直接进入一个新的角色。

**〔V3｜不是修补旧我，而是成为新我〕**

The best way to change is not to mend old you, but to become a fresh you. Good luck, my friend.
> 改变最好的方式，不是修补旧我，而是成为一个全新的自己。祝你好运，我的朋友。
