## Part A｜核心论点（逐句对照）

**EN** "Right person, wrong time" is the most comfortable story you can tell yourself — and the most paralyzing one.

> “对的人，错的时间”是你能讲给自己听的最舒服的故事，也是最让人停步的故事。

**EN** It hands the blame to the calendar, so you never have to examine your own willingness.

> 它把责任推给日历，于是你永远不必审视自己到底愿不愿意。

**EN** Timing is never abstract. It is the sum of concrete conditions — work, study, distance, how often you can actually show up.

> 时间从来不是抽象的。它是具体条件的总和——工作、读书、距离、你到底能出现几次。

**EN** So "the timing isn't right" usually translates into "I'm not willing to rearrange my life for this."

> 所以“时机不对”通常翻译过来是：“我不愿意为这件事重新安排我的生活。”

**EN** The right person is not someone who fits only the best season of your life.

> 对的人，不是那个只适配你人生最好季节的人。

**EN** The right person is someone willing to fight, to face the imperfect season with you.

> 对的人，是愿意和你一起争取、一起面对不完美季节的人。

**EN** The test is choice, not compatibility.

> 检验标准是选择，不是契合。

**EN** Compatibility is an adjective. Choice is a verb.

> 契合是形容词，选择是动词。

**EN** If the timing is always the problem, then the timing was never the problem.

> 如果时机永远是问题，那时机就从来不是问题。

**EN** Maybe the choice was never strong enough.

> 也许那个选择，从来就不够坚定。

## Part B｜论证展开（整段对照）

There is a reason this phrase survives every breakup. It is unfalsifiable. "I don't have enough money" can be disproved — you will always find someone with less who made it work anyway. "The timing is off" cannot be disproved. You can only wait. And any reason that cannot be proved wrong will get reused over and over, across relationships, jobs, and projects. Listen for it in your own mouth — it usually shows up at the exact moment you have already decided.

> 这句话之所以能穿越每一次分手存活下来，是因为它无法被证伪。“我钱不够”是可以被反驳的——你总能找到比你更窘迫、却依然做成了的人。而“时机不对”无法反驳，你只能等。任何一个无法被证伪的理由，都会被反复征用——在感情里、在工作里、在项目里。留意它什么时候从你自己嘴里冒出来；那通常正是你其实已经做出决定的时刻。

You cannot ask "why is the timing wrong." There is no answer to that, only waiting. Ask a different question instead: what have I actually rearranged for this? That question has an answer. It is uncomfortable, it is specific, and it is something you can act on today. The same predicament stops being a state you endure and becomes an object you can work on.

> 你没法问“为什么时机不对”——那没有答案，只有等待。换一个问题问：我到底为它重新安排过什么？这个问题有答案。它令人不适、非常具体，而且今天就能动手。同一个困境，就从一种你只能忍受的状态，变成了一个你可以处理的对象。

The video's definition of the right person is worth borrowing precisely because it is an *inclusion* test, not a *compatibility* test. Anyone can fit into your best season — that requires nothing from them. Staying through the season where you are broke, exhausted, or unavailable — that requires a decision, made repeatedly.

> 视频里对“对的人”的定义值得借用，正因为它是一道**纳入测试**，而不是**匹配测试**。任何人都能适配你最好的季节——那不需要他付出任何东西。而你破产、疲惫、分身乏术的那些季节里，还留下来——那需要一个被反复做出的决定。

### C.2 — Unfalsifiable excuses are the expensive ones

A falsifiable excuse gets corrected by reality; you learn, and you move. An unfalsifiable one just sits there, available forever, collecting interest. "I need more experience" is checkable. "I'm not ready yet" is not. Prefer the checkable version of your own reason whenever you can find one — it at least tells you what to do next.

<details>
<summary>中文自查</summary>

可证伪的借口会被现实纠正，你因此学到东西并往前动。不可证伪的借口只是待在那儿，永久可用，而且还在收利息。“我需要更多经验”是可检验的。“我还没准备好”不是。只要找得到，就尽量把自己的理由换成可检验的版本——它至少能告诉你下一步做什么。

</details>

### C.3 — Move the question from *time* to *choice*

*Why is the timing wrong?* has no answer and no action attached. *What have I rearranged for this?* has both. The first question turns you into someone waiting for conditions to improve. The second turns you into someone who can decide today — including deciding, honestly, to walk away.

## 中文速览落地清单

1. **做一次借口清点。** 写下最近三个月用“时机/条件不成熟”解释过的 3 件事，逐条改写成“我选择暂时不做，因为 ______”。填得出来的，放下；填不出来或填了心虚的，那是优先级问题，不是条件问题。
2. **给最心虚的那件事定一个不可再退的日期。** 到点要么做最小可行的动作，要么正式宣布放弃——不允许再挂在那儿收利息。
3. **只问动作层面的问题。** 不谈“合不合适”（状态），改问“这件事上你愿意做什么”（动作）。状态可以自我安慰，动作不能。
4. **识别自己嘴里的“时机不对”。** 它出现的那一刻，通常你已经做出了决定，只是还没承认。
