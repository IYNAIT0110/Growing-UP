## Part B｜整段对照

### B.1 视频 1（前半）

I'm telling you, sooner or later you're going to have to understand this. If two people are going to have a long, comfortable relationship, it rests on what they share and on attraction. Not on what you're doing right now — giving and giving, in a moral glow of your own sacrifice.

> 你迟早有一天你要明白：人和人之间如果有长久舒适的关系，靠的是共性和吸引，不是像你现在这个样子——一味地付出，道德式的自我感动。

### B.2 视频 1（后半）

Do you know what this thing between you two actually is? What it's called? It's called debasing yourself.

> 你知道你和他这种方式算什么吗？这叫什么吗？犯贱。

### B.3 视频 2（前半）

Good is the minimum. It's the baseline. You have to be so much more than good. And even if you're great and lucky, you still have to work really fucking hard. And even that is not enough.

> 好只是及格线，是底线。你必须是远远超过"好"的东西。而且就算你很出色、运气又好，你还是得拼命干。而就算那样，也还不够。

### B.4 视频 2（后半）

You have to scratch and claw and it never fucking ends. And it doesn't get better. It just gets harder. So don't complain to me that I'm making your life hard. You don't even know what that means.

> 你得连抓带扒地往上挣，而这永远不会结束。它不会变好，它只会更难。所以别跟我抱怨说我让你日子难过——你根本不知道那是什么意思。

### B.5 视频 3（前半）

When you can't let someone go, look at how you were let go. When he loved you, one word from you could buy a whole night of him soothing you.

> 放不下别人的时候，想想自己是怎么被别人放弃的。他爱你的时候，因为一句话都能哄你一晚上。

### B.6 视频 3（后半）

When he didn't, a whole night of tears couldn't buy a single word. So what is there left for you to regret?

> 不爱你的时候，你掉一晚上眼泪也换不来一句话。所以你还有什么好遗憾的。

### B.7 视频 4（前半）

Being misunderstood is a sign of having a lot of life in you. Because first it goes through being misunderstood, then through being accepted, then through being misunderstood again. That shows it's something with a great deal of life in it.

> 被误解代表的就是生命力强。因为它有被误解的过程，然后它又会有被接受的过程，也有再被误解的过程——这证明它是一个非常有生命力的东西。

### B.8 视频 4（后半）

Whether it's the music, or the person. So I like being misunderstood.

> 不管是音乐还是这个人而已。所以我喜欢被误解。
