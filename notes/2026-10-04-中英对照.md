**EN** When you haven't met enough people, it's very easy to become self-centered — to judge others by your own values and your own experience.

> 每个人能承受的重量不一样，不要用自己的想象去轻易评价别人经历的人生。

**EN** I don't care who is doing better than me, because the truth is, I'm not in competition with anyone else.
> 我不在意谁比我做得更好，因为事实是，我没有在跟任何人竞争。

**EN** The only person I need to be better than is the person I was yesterday.
> 我唯一需要超越的人，是昨天的自己。

**EN** Every day, I wake up ready to push myself further, to challenge my limits, and become the best version of myself.
> 每天醒来，我都准备好把自己推得更远，去挑战我的极限，成为最好的自己。

## Part B · 原文整段

**〔V1｜自我中心是怎么长出来的〕**

When you haven't met enough people, it's very easy to become self-centered — to judge others by your own values and your own experience. That makes you narrow. It's suffocating. You label people and pass judgment on them very easily.
> 你见的人不够多的时候，你非常容易自我中心，就是按我的价值观、按我的见识判断别人。所以他非常狭隘，他特别窒息，就是你很容易给别人贴标签、做判断。

**〔V1｜标准不是唯一正确的〕**

I've seen many scenes and met many people you'd never come across in daily life. When you talk with these people and try to feel what their life is like, you begin to understand that your standards are not the only correct ones. Everyone can carry a different amount of weight. Don't use your own imagination to casually judge the life someone else has lived.
> 我见到了很多我们日常生活是见不到的、那种场景和人。当你跟这些人交谈，去体会他的生存的时候，你才明白你的标准不是唯一正确的。每个人能承受的重量不一样，不要用自己的想象去轻易评价别人经历的人生。

**〔V2｜换掉坐标系〕**

I don't care who is doing better than me, because the truth is, I'm not in competition with anyone else. The only person I need to be better than is the person I was yesterday. Last year, I was different. I've grown, improved, and learned a lot. That's what really matters. It's me versus me.
> 我不在意谁比我做得更好，因为事实是，我没有在跟任何人竞争。我唯一需要超越的人，是昨天的自己。去年我是另一个样子。我成长了、进步了，也学到了很多。这才是真正重要的。是我和我自己比。

**〔V2｜别人的成功不是我的失败证据〕**

Every day, I wake up ready to push myself further, to challenge my limits, and become the best version of myself. I don't let other people's success make me feel small. Their journey is theirs, and mine is mine.
> 每天醒来，我都准备好把自己推得更远，去挑战我的极限，成为最好的自己。我不会因为别人的成功而觉得自己渺小。他们的路是他们的，我的是我的。

**〔V3｜去试〕**

Try something. You may succeed. You may fail. But make it your life's work to remake the world, because there is nothing more beautiful or worthwhile than working to leave something better for humanity.
> 去试一件事。你可能会成功，也可能会失败。但要把重塑世界当成你一生的事业，因为没有什么比为人类留下更好的东西更美好、更值得。

**〔V3｜别犯过于谨慎的错〕**

Whatever you do, don't make the mistake of being too cautious. Don't assume that by staying put, the ground won't move beneath your feet. The status quo simply won't last.
> 无论你做什么，都不要犯"过于谨慎"的错。别以为原地不动，脚下的地就不会移动。现状根本不会持续。

**〔V4｜被抛弃不等于失败〕**

You have to remember: not being chosen — or being left behind — does not equal failure. The harm the other person did to you, and your own fixation, can keep you from letting go for two months, three months, or even longer. You know perfectly well that the thing itself isn't that important.
> 你一定要记住，你没被选择、又或者被抛弃，它不等于失败。对方带给你的伤害、你的执念，让你两个月、三个月甚至更久都忘不掉。你也明知道这个事情本身其实没有那么重要。

**〔V4｜痛苦等于疼痛乘以抗拒〕**

You define the end of this relationship as proof that you are a failure. In your world you don't allow yourself to fail; you don't allow yourself to be hurt. So you don't allow it to end. Suffering equals pain multiplied by resistance. The only answer is acceptance — allowing. I've said it many times: we have to change the way we think.
> 你把这段关系的结束定义为自己就是一个失败者，在你的世界里你不允许自己失败，不允许自己受伤、痛。所以你就不允许它结束。痛苦就等于疼痛和抗拒的叠加。唯一的答案就是接受、允许。我说过很多遍了，我们一定要转变思维。

**〔V4｜哪部分是别人的决定〕**

He is simply someone who eats from his bowl while eyeing the pot. He is perfectly capable of being unkind, of not loving you anymore; he is capable of deciding that someone new is worth more than you. The person you fought so hard to keep can slip into someone else's hands very easily — with no explanation, no reason given, because it was entirely his decision.
> 对方他就是一个吃着碗里看着锅里的人，他就是可以很坏、不爱你了，他就是觉得新的人比你有价值。你拼命争取的人，就是可以很轻易地落到别人手里，没有任何说法，没有任何解释，因为这全是对方的决定。

**〔V4｜哪部分才是我的课题〕**

It was never your task to begin with. But if you can't let it go, if you keep obsessing and asking ten thousand whys — that is your task. You're the one making it impossible for yourself to move on; you're hurting and refusing to accept at the same time. That is repeatedly putting yourself on the rack.
> 这压根就不是你的课题。但如果你想不通，你一直纠结，一直十万个为什么，这就是你的课题了。你就是跟自己过不去，你又痛苦又拒绝接受，这等于反复给你自己上刑。

**〔V4｜结束与失败是两件事〕**

Remember this well: an ending and a failure are two completely different things. You really did not fail. A flower withers not because it didn't try hard enough, but because the season has come and its cycle is complete.
> 一定要记好了，结束和失败是完全不同的两件事情，你真的没有失败。花朵凋零，并不是因为它不够努力，而是季节到了，它的周期完成了。

**〔V4｜站在自己这边〕**

Don't take your filter, take your ideal, and use it to torture the you who lives in reality. You can love someone and trust someone without reservation — you gave 120% sincerity — and yet you won't even allow yourself to be hurt, won't allow yourself to be sad. You are too hard on yourself. If you won't even stand on your own side, then you will have no strength left to face the world.
> 不要拿着滤镜、拿着你的理想去折磨现实当中的自己。你可以毫无保留地去爱一个人、相信一个人，你付出 120% 的真诚，但你连允许自己受伤、允许自己难过都不行——你对自己太苛刻了。你自己都不站在你自己这边，那你必然没有心力去对抗这个世界。

**〔V4｜目标与告别〕**

So we need to use the right method, to see everything from a new angle. Our goal is to cross the swamp, not to beat the crocodile in it. All right — remember to love yourself.
> 所以我们要用正确的方法、用新的视角去看待一切。我们的目标是穿过泥潭，而不是打赢泥潭里的鳄鱼。好吧，记得爱自己。

**〔V5｜我不适合夏天和冬天〕**

Autumn is all I've got. I haven't got the body for summer or the mind that's needed for winter or even the heart that unfolds in spring — but fall, I think, is where the soul clings.
> 秋天是我仅有的一切。我没有夏天的身体，没有过冬需要的头脑，甚至连在春天舒展的心都没有——但我觉得，秋天才是灵魂赖着不走的季节。

**〔V5｜秋天允许什么〕**

I find it here every time, looking for me along with a chill that nips at the skin but reminds me that I'm warm-blooded and flooded with hot bright color. It's a season when dying things are allowed to be beautiful and living things allowed to be ugly and everything allowed to be still. Autumn gives permission — and that is what we've got. That is it.
> 每一次我都能在这里寻回它，它也在寻找我，伴随着一丝刺在皮肤上的寒意——这寒意提醒我，我是热血的，并且被鲜亮炽烈的颜色灌满。这是一个允许枯萎的事物绚烂、允许鲜活的事物难看、允许一切停下来的季节。秋天给出的是许可——而这就是我们拥有的东西。就这样。

**〔V6｜男人与女人的两套逻辑〕**

Men are amorous but long-lasting; women are deeply affectionate but ruthless. Women have no love — whoever treats her well, she goes with him. Men are different: they treat someone well only when they like her. That's why you think men are more likely to treat love as a faith, while women are more likely to treat love as a choice.
> 男人多情而长情，女人深情而绝情。女人是没有爱情的，谁对她好她就跟谁走，而男人是他喜欢谁才会对谁好。所以你认为男人更容易把爱情当信仰，女人更容易把爱情当选择。

**〔V6｜爱是一种权利关系〕**

But from a woman's point of view, love is never pure love; it is a relationship of power. So when a woman finds that this man can no longer provide the value she needs, she withdraws quickly. In real life, many men, even when they have an affair, still hesitate and go back and forth. But once a woman has an affair, she tends to leave decisively.
> 但在女人看来，爱从来不是纯爱，而是一种权利关系。所以当女人发现这个男人无法继续提供他所需要的价值，她就会迅速撤离。在现实生活中，很多男人即便有了婚外恋情，可能还犹犹豫豫、反复折腾。但是一个女人有了婚外的情感，往往会毅然决然地离开。

**〔V6｜情感锚点转移〕**

For a woman, forgetting someone is simple: she only needs another man to appear. A few sweet words, a movie, a meal, and she'll forget you completely. In psychology this behavior is called emotional anchor transfer. When she projects her attachment, expectations, and sense of security onto someone new, the old anchor is cut off quickly. In this process the new partner is promoted and the ex is demoted. Once this psychological detachment is complete, she exits for good without looking back.
> 对女人来说，想忘掉一个人很简单，只需要另外一个男人的出现。多几句甜言蜜语，带她看一场电影，再吃个饭，她就会把你忘得干干净净。从心理学上看，女人的这种行为被叫做情感锚点转移。当她把依恋、期待、安全感全部投射到新的人身上时，旧的锚点就会被快速切断。在这个过程中，新的伴侣会被提升，前任则被降级。一旦完成这种心理脱离，她就会头也不回地彻底退出。

**〔V6｜男人的放下〕**

Men, on the surface, seem strong, but when a man truly falls in love with a woman he becomes defenceless. Because a man's letting go is essentially a process of killing himself. He has to overturn all of his past investment — and the more he invested, the harder it is for him to walk away.
> 而男人看似强大，但当他真正爱上一个女人时，会变得不堪一击。因为男人的放下，本质上是一个杀死自己的过程。他必须要推翻自己过往所有的付出，而他付出的越多，就越没办法轻易地抽身。

**〔V6｜一项追踪研究〕**

Psychology has also done research on this: experts tracked a hundred couples who once loved each other but eventually separated. They found that the longer they were apart, the more the men missed their former partners, while the women tended to let go of the past more easily. So after a relationship breaks down, the probability of a man coming back is far higher than that of a woman.
> 心理学领域也曾做过这样一个研究：专家追踪了一百对曾经相爱但最后分开了的伴侣。然后发现随着分开的时间越长，男人会更加想念曾经的伴侣，但是女人往往更容易放下过去。所以一段关系破裂以后，男人回头的概率远远比女人要高。

**〔V6｜一次性的绝情〕**

And once a woman has let go of this one and has the next one, it is almost impossible for her to love the old one again — because a woman's ruthlessness is a one-time thing. A woman's genes determine her instinct to be drawn to the strong; when she sees a stronger man, she will fall for someone else. Because she is drawn to the strong, her turning away is very ruthless.
> 而女人一旦放下了这一任，有了下一任，几乎不可能再爱上旧人，因为女人的绝情是一次性的。女人的基因决定了她慕强的本能；当她看到一个更强大的男人，她就会移情别恋。因为她慕强，所以她转身是很无情的。

**〔V6｜收束〕**

So why do we say that in a relationship, a man saying he wants to separate doesn't necessarily mean it's over — but once a woman's heart is dead, the outcome is almost irreversible.
> 所以为什么我们说一段关系，男人说要分开未必会结束；而女人一旦心死，结果几乎不可逆。
