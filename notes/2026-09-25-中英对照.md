## Part A｜逐句对照（英文为译写）

### A.1 视频 1

**EN** What's the best way to walk away from a relationship?

> **中** 放弃一段感情最好的方式是什么？

**EN** Honestly? As long as you put your hand on your heart and can say, "I did my best" — that's enough.

> **中** 老实讲啊，只要你摸着自己的良心，说一句"我尽力了"，就足够了。

### A.2 视频 2

**EN** Sometimes I think that if this person treats me badly, it's because I gave him the right to.

> **中** 我有时候会觉得，如果这个人对我不好，是因为我赋予了他这个权利。

**EN** What I need to do is take that right back.

> **中** 我要做的是收回这个权利。

**EN** Don't you think bad habits are just a product of being spoiled?

> **中** 你不觉得毛病都是惯出来的吗？

**EN** Because sometimes — honestly, I love you, so I gave you this key, this right.

> **中** 因为有时候，其实——我爱你，我就给了你这个钥匙、这个权利。

**EN** Right. That's something I picked up from a novel I read way back.

> **中** 对，这我当初看《群老小说》看来的。

**EN** What he meant is that I keep no defences up against you.

> **中** 他的意思是，我对你是不设防的。

**EN** I love you, so I gave it to you — including the right to hurt me.

> **中** 我爱你，我就给了你，包括伤害我的权利。

**EN** And there's something to that, actually.

> **中** 就这话其实是有一些道理的。

**EN** Which means: take that right back.

> **中** 那就是要收回这个权利。

**EN** Take it back — decisively. That's the hard part.

> **中** 果断地收回，很难。
