### 整段

当然，你还是可以选择带着"有期待、有回报"这个形态去看任何一段感情，这是你的自由，没有对错。但如果你选择了这条路，你就要愿赌服输，不是吗？赢，是你的好运；亏了，也不要怨天尤人。毕竟投资是有风险的嘛。入市需谨慎，记住这句话。

> Of course, you can still choose to look at any relationship through the lens of expectation and return. That's your freedom, and there's no right or wrong about it. But if you pick that road, you have to accept the outcome of the bet, don't you? If you win, that's your good luck; if you lose, don't blame the world. Investing carries risk, after all. Enter the market with caution — remember that line.

所以下次你要去帮忙的时候，先问问看自己：如果这个人什么都没有还给我，什么回应都不给我，我还愿不愿意帮？如果是愿意，那你就去做，勇敢地去，而且是做到底。然后做好之后就不要再惦记。Steady 一点，潇洒一点，不要去想。因为你已经得到答案了：就是你想做，所以你做好，心里面已经很舒服了，不是吗？

> So next time you're about to help someone, ask yourself first: if this person gives me nothing back and offers no response at all, do I still want to help? If the answer is yes, then go do it — go bravely, and see it through to the end. And once it's done, stop thinking about it. Be steady, be easy about it, let it go. Because you already have your answer: you wanted to do it, so doing it already felt good, didn't it?

真的，人际关系最舒服的状态从来不是"我对你多好，你就要对我多好"，而是"我对你好是我愿意，你对我好是我幸运"。真的，你这辈子控制不了别人。你只可以——记住，永远——可以控制的人是你自己：要不要付出、付出多少，全靠你自己去决定。

> Really, the most comfortable state in any relationship was never "I'm this good to you, so you must be this good to me." It's "my being good to you is because I want to; your being good to me is my good fortune." Really, you can't control other people in this life. The only person you can ever control — remember, ever — is yourself: whether to give, and how much, is entirely up to you.
