## Part B · 原文整段

At first, the spirit is a camel. It takes all the "shoulds" the world hands it and carries them. Family expectations, social standards, templates for success — they pile onto the camel's back like stones. The camel's greatness is its capacity to bear weight. It believes that the more it carries and the longer it endures, the more substance its life will have. But that is exactly where the camel's problem lies: the values it carries were never its own choice. It mistakes what others call "should" for what it always wanted.

> **中** 最初，精神像骆驼。它把外界给它的"应该"全部接过来。比如家庭期待、社会标准、成功模板，它们像一块块石头，被骆驼接过来背在背上。骆驼的伟大在于能负重，它相信只要背得够多、忍得够久，生命就有分量。但骆驼的问题也在这里：它背负的价值不是自己选择的，它把别人认为的"应该"误认为这是自己本来就要的。

In the second transformation, the spirit becomes a lion. The lion claims its freedom with "I will": it criticises, refuses, rebels. It kills the old values and takes back its own will. This matters enormously, because without rebellion a person never leaves the camel's state. But the lion has its limits too: it can shatter the old world, yet it cannot build a new one. It knows what it does not want, but not necessarily what it does want. If a person stays in the lion stage forever, hatred and resistance will define them. The more they wrestle with something, the more that thing occupies their spirit.

> **中** 第二变，精神变成狮子。狮子用"我要"来争夺自由，它批判、拒绝、反抗，杀死旧价值，夺回自己的意志。这非常重要，因为没有反抗，人永远走不出骆驼状态。但狮子也有局限：它能打碎旧世界，却不能建造新世界；它知道自己不要什么，却未必知道自己要什么。如果一个人永远停在狮子阶段，他会不断被仇恨和反抗定义。他越跟什么较劲，什么就越占据他的精神。

In the third transformation, the spirit becomes a child — the state Nietzsche considered most ideal. A child is a creative being. A child builds a sandcastle not because someone is grading it, but because it wants to create. The innocence of the child is not ignorance; it is no longer being polluted by old values. Forgetting is not escaping the past; it is refusing to let the past decide the present. A child creates value rather than receiving it; it can start over rather than stay stuck in an old ledger. A child's freedom is not "nobody tells me what to do"; it is "I can give my own life meaning". It is a lawgiver to itself. Yet none of the three is higher than the others. Without the camel, a person has no capacity to bear; without the lion, no courage to break free; without the child, no possibility of rebirth. The ideal state is to hold the camel's strength, the lion's courage, and the child's creativity together — so that a person can carry the weight they chose themselves, refuse values that are not theirs, and create a game that belongs to them.

> **中** 第三变，变成孩子，这是尼采认为最理想的状态。孩子是一种创造性的存在。孩子堆沙堡，不是因为有人给他打分，而是因为他愿意创造。这里孩子代表的天真不是无知，是不再被旧价值污染；遗忘不是逃避过去，是不让过去决定现在。孩子在创造价值，而不是接受价值；他能重新开始，而不是永远困在旧账里。孩子的自由不是"没人管我"，是"我能为自己的生命赋予意义"，它是自我的立法者。但这三者之间没有谁更高等。没有骆驼，人没有承担的能力；没有狮子，人没有挣脱的勇气；没有孩子，人没有新生的可能。理想的状态应是把骆驼的力量、狮子的勇气、孩子的创造，都合在一起，让人能够承担自己选择的重负，能够拒绝不属于自己的价值，并且能够创造属于自己的游戏。
